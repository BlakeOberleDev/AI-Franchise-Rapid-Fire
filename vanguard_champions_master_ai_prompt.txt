SECTION 1: CORE FRANCHISE GAMEPLAY PILLARS
================================================================================

Eleven of the thirteen games (Games 1–6, 7–11, and 13) are built on the signature VANGUARD CHAMPIONS TACTICAL ENGINE—a blend of Kingdom Rush lane-defense and Battleheart real-time squad micro-management. Game 12 (Rebirth) uses a dedicated TOP-DOWN ACTION-ADVENTURE RPG ENGINE (and Game 1 / Game 13 extend the tactical engine with unique era mechanics).

Every tactical Vanguard Champions game MUST strictly implement these four pillars:

1. SQUAD & UNIT CONTROL (Battleheart-Style Micro)
   - 6-Hero Squad: Before each stage, the player selects a customizable squad of 6 heroes from that game's roster (each hero has a distinct role: Tank, Bruiser, Ranger/Archer, Mage, Healer/Cleric, Assassin/Specialist, plus 1–2 active cooldown skills and 1 passive).
   - Movable Expendable Troops: Alongside the 6 heroes, players deploy act-specific, expendable default troops (e.g., Pikemen, Archers, Elven Swordsmen, Dwarven Deep-Wardens, Ancient Mystics, etc.) via battlefield reinforcement cooldowns or gold/energy. Unlike traditional tower defense barracks units tethered to a rally flag, these troops are 100% FREELY MOVABLE anywhere across the map via click-drag or select-and-move, just like heroes.
   - Respawn Timers (No Permadeath): When a hero's HP reaches 0, they do not die permanently. They enter a punishing respawn timer (e.g., 15–25 seconds, scaling slightly with wave tension) at the rally point. Losing a main tank or healer creates a dramatic tempo swing rather than an instant game over.
   - Controls: Intuitive mouse drag-from-unit-to-destination (Battleheart style) AND click-to-select + right-click/click-to-move, plus drag-box multi-select, hotkeys `1`–`6` for heroes, `Q`/`W`/`E` for active skills, `Space` for tactical pause/slow-motion (crucial for browser playability), and full touch-event support for mobile/tablet browsers.

2. ZERO-DAMAGE SUPPORT TOWERS
   - 3-Tower Loadout: Before each stage, the player picks a loadout of 3 support tower types (from 5–6 unlocked/available options per game) to construct on tactical build pads along the lanes.
   - STRICT ZERO-DAMAGE RULE: Towers NEVER deal direct HP damage to enemies. They are pure force multipliers. Your 6 heroes and movable expendable troops do 100% of the killing.
   - Support Tower Functions:
     * Ally Buffs: Attack speed aura, armor/shield ward, healing beacon, cooldown-haste obelisk, troop-rally speed banner.
     * Enemy Debuffs: Frost/mud slow field, armor-shredding hex spire, damage-amplification curse, silence/anti-caster obelisk, blind/miss cloud.
     * Space & Flow Control: Physical choke-point barricade/wall, knockback/repulsion pulse pylon, ally teleport/warp pad, stealth-reveal watchtower.
   - Upgrades: Towers can be upgraded (Tier 1 -> Tier 2 -> Tier 3) during a stage using wave gold to increase radius, potency, or unlock secondary utility effects—still dealing zero direct damage.

3. COMBAT & ANTI-UNIT ENEMY COUNTERPLAY
   - Dedicated Anti-Unit Archetypes: Enemy waves MUST include specialized units designed to punish static clumping and shred unattended expendable troops:
     * Cleavers / Siege Crushers: Heavy telegraphing AoE slams that devastate tightly clumped squads—forcing players to split and reposition.
     * Shredders / Piercers: Fast multi-hit or piercing-line enemies that melt expendable frontline troops if tanks/control towers don't intercept them.
     * Backline Divers / Assassins / Leap-Beasts: Bypass the front line to hunt healers and mages, forcing active peeling.
     * Disruptors / Casters: Silence heroes, buff enemy armor, or drop ground hazard zones that force constant movement.
   - Telegraphing: All major enemy AoE attacks and boss abilities MUST display crisp ground telegraphs (red/amber circles, cones, or lines) with a 1.0–1.8s windup so skilled players can dodge by dragging units out of danger.

4. FLAT PROGRESSION (NO STAR-UPGRADE TREES)
   - Do NOT implement grindy meta-progression star-upgrade trees (+5% HP, +10% damage, etc.).
   - Stage balance must remain tight, flat, and skill-based around hero kit synergies, troop positioning, active ability timing, and 3-tower support loadouts.
   - Progression across a campaign unlocks new heroes, new support tower types, act-specific troop types, and Lore Codex entries—never raw stat inflation.

SECTION 3: CANONICAL LORE BIBLE ("THE RECORDS OF ALL TIME")
================================================================================

All story briefings, in-game dialogue, character names, and Codex entries MUST remain faithful to this canonical chronology:

- THE AGE OF THE PRIMORDIALS: Created by the First Born, two primeval beings existed in separate realms: The Harbinger (chaos/destruction, in the Void) and The Wellspring (pure energy, in the Bloom). Millennia of war caused their realms to collide; the Harbinger consumed the Wellspring, who later burst forth, restoring the Bloom and Void and creating a third physical realm: The Emergence. Critically wounded, the Wellspring split into four Primordial Elemental Entities—Earth, Air, Fire, and Water—to balance reality and block the Void, while the Wellspring went missing. Ancient civilizations repeatedly rose and were wiped out by elemental cycles until 14 ancient humans fled into deep caves and found two Orbs: a radiant Light Orb (Elemental power) and a consuming Dark Orb (Arcane power). The first touched the Light Orb and vanished (known in legend as Harethel the Light); the second touched the Dark Orb and vanished unnamed.
- THE AGE OF THE ANCIENTS: The remaining 12 learned to channel the Orbs' infinite power into crystals—6 devoting themselves to Light (creation) and 6 to Dark (destruction so newness can follow). They built civilization and eventually ascended into the 12 Gods. In their absence, Gildelal and his apprentice Gimelal led a civil war, enslaved humanity to mine crystals, and created orcs, goblins, and savage races. Gildelal ascended as a powerful deity and Gimelal as a minor one. The 12 Gods returned, fought a massive war, banished Gildelal to the Void, and drove Gimelal into hiding in the Far East. The 12 Gods then waged war against the four Primordial Elementals, siphoning 3/4 of each elemental's power into 4 massive Crystals hidden across the land, intending to banish the weakened elementals and anchor reality to the Crystals. During the ensuing golden age, the Gods created the modern races (modern humans, elves, dwarves). Suddenly, Lord Erebus—the unnamed 14th human, now the Harbinger's apprentice—tore open a Void rift. The 12 Gods summoned Harethel the Light from the Light Orb to fight Erebus for millennia. Simultaneously, the Fire Entity reunited with his Crystal near the Temple of the Orbs; the Mystic Keepers defeated him and banished him to a surface volcano, though the Temple was destroyed and the surviving Keepers fled to hide the Fire Crystal, losing the Orbs' location to time. Harethel ended the war by banishing herself and Erebus to the Void. The Ancients died out, magic began fading, and the 4 Crystals were never activated.
- THE AGE OF KINGS: Centuries later, kingdoms rose. The Fire Entity marched from his volcano against Alliston and Lorentil, forging their historic alliance alongside Elves, Dwarves, and a mysterious northern Ice Entity. Together they banished Fire to the Void—unknowingly breaking the first anchor of reality. Soon after, Gimelal returned from the Far East with depleted magic, leading evil men, orcs, and goblins. Alliston rode East to aid Lorentil; amid reality-fracture fire anomalies, their greatest mages drained Gimelal's divinity and killed him for good.
- THE PRIMORDIAL USURPING: The Ice Entity betrayed the alliance and invaded Alliston. Prince Arryn and his cohorts fought their way to Lorentil, rallied a counterattack, defeated the Ice Entity, and melted the northern glaciers. Thawed from the ice, an ancient Dino King and prehistoric beast-riders overran Alliston; Lorentil pushed through the Sleeping Mountains and Dwarven tunnels to liberate Alliston. Years later, southern seaports flooded as Atlanteans invaded; the alliance discovered the Ice Entity was actually the Primordial Water Entity—now melted and controlling the Atlanteans—and summoned ancient mages to banish Water to the Void. Seeing Fire and Water banished, Earth and Air formed a desperate truce; the alliance fought into the high peaks and skylands to banish both. Finally, all four Elementals combined in the Void to tear back into reality in a massive Maelstrom, until heroes from across the realm united to drain and seal them in the Void.
- THE AGE OF REBIRTH: With all four elements removed, reality fractured—landmasses floated into the sky and chaos reigned. Four adventurers followed ancient scrolls across the shattered world to locate and activate the 4 Elemental Crystals, stumbling upon the lost Light and Dark Orbs deep in the fractured depths.
- THE APOCALYPSE: All entities in the Void (the 4 Elementals, Gildelal, Lord Erebus, Harethel, and the Harbinger) merged into one Arcane-Elemental Entity to erase the Emergence. The 12 Gods returned, mages expended all remaining crystal power, and the Orbs from Rebirth were used to pull Harethel free to split the Entity. Four canonical ending branches exist (all 4 playable in Game 13):
  1. Ending A (Restoration): The split restores the Wellspring, who banishes the Harbinger; the 4 Crystals stabilize reality in permanent balance.
  2. Ending B (The Eternal Cycle): The Wellspring and Harbinger burst forth with cataclysmic force, obliterating the Emergence and resetting the Bloom and Void to the beginning of time.
  3. Ending C (The Mentor's Return): Harethel summons the living Wellspring—who had been mentoring her inside the Bloom—to personally cast the Harbinger back into the Void.
  4. Ending D (The Unified Orb): The Wellspring is truly gone; a final battle banishes the combined Entity into a merged Bloom-Void realm (fusing the Orbs), while Harethel remains in the Emergence with the 12 Gods as eternal guardian.
